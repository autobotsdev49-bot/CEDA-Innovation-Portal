16 desktop mockups (ML-01 to ML-16)

5 grouped visual boards, plus static HTML/CSS files for frontend reference.

This is a PROPOSED MANAGERIAL ASSESSOR ROLE, not a claim about CEDA's current internal permissions. Managers may allocate assessors within delegated scope, check conflicts, monitor and quality-assure assessment submissions, compare independent evaluator scores, record moderation, manage authorized clarification requests, and forward consolidated recommendations. Managers do NOT silently edit an assessor's individual score or publish a final applicant outcome. Final approval/decline and release are separately authorized steps.

The hackathon brief specifies six assessment categories each scored from 1–5 with comments: Strategic Alignment; Innovation & Originality; Impact Potential; Feasibility & Viability; Scalability & Sustainability; Implementation Capacity, Team Capability & Governance. No weightings are defined. Do not implement automatic weighted scoring or guarantee any thresholds from these mockups.

All figures, names, statuses, dates and application numbers in the illustrations are demo data. Designs and HTML are static, not functioning applications.
