CEDA INNOVATION PORTAL — SUPPORT OFFICER UI CONCEPTS

14 desktop screens (SO-01 to SO-14) and 4 mobile screens (SOM-01 to SOM-04), 5 overview boards, and matching static HTML references.

Roles: Support officers handle AI-to-human handoffs, tickets, live conversations and safe referrals. Their access to application data is restricted and not granted by knowing a reference. Final evaluation and decision release are separate roles. AI knowledge publication requires authorized review; officers may suggest edits.

Names, dates, example tickets, counts, and times are DEMO DATA. These are proposed hackathon UX concepts and not official CEDA operating processes. The HTML is static visual reference, not a functioning app.
